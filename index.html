FAQ

Good questions.

How is RideCast different from a weather app?

A weather app gives you the raw forecast. RideCast reads it the way a rider would, checks every hour against your own limits, and gives you a score and a clear verdict.

Is it only for cyclists?

No. RideCast works for cyclists and motorbike riders, and scores the weather differently for each.

What does it look at?

Wind speed, gusts, rain chance and amount, temperature and feels-like, hour by hour. Commuter Mode also checks the wind direction against your route.

Does it work where I live?

RideCast is built for New Zealand and works for any town or city you choose. More countries are on the way.

Is it free?

Yes. The daily score, forecast, alerts and ride log are free. Commuter Mode is an optional upgrade for people who ride to work and back.

Do I need an account?

No account and no login. Your ride log stays on your phone.

Privacy, terms & support

Privacy Policy+

Last updated: 21 June 2026

RideCast (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what the RideCast app collects and why.

Information We Collect

The RideCast app does not require an account and does not ask you to log in. We collect only what’s needed to provide forecasts and notifications:

  • The location you choose — the city or route you select in the app. RideCast does not collect precise GPS location. Your selection is used only to fetch relevant weather forecasts.
  • Device notification token — a technical identifier used to deliver your daily ride notifications.
  • Your settings — notification times, route details, and rider preferences.
  • Forecast feedback — if you rate whether a forecast was accurate, that rating is sent to us to improve the service. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Subscription status — if you purchase Commuter Mode, your subscription state is managed by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Email address — only if you contact us for support or sign up for updates on our website.

Your personal ride log (the days you rode) is stored on your device, not on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Deliver ride scores and weather-based insights for the location you choose
  • Send the daily ride notifications you’ve enabled
  • Provide and improve the RideCast service
  • Respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted third-party services only to operate the app:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast data
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications
  • RevenueCat and Apple — subscription management and payments

These services only process data as needed to run RideCast.

Data Storage

Your data is stored using secure, industry-standard practices.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information we hold at any time.
